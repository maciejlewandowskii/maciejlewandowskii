### 👨‍💻 About Me

I am a Mid Backend Developer, creative coder, and freelancer specializing in architecting and developing robust web systems. Running my own freelance business since 2018, I have extensive experience delivering reliable backend solutions and managing custom software projects. 

- 💻 I specialize in the **PHP** ecosystem, primarily focusing on **Laravel** and **Symfony**.
- 🚀 Recently built **[Spatial Photo API](https://github.com/maciejlewandowskii/spatial-photo-api)**, a high-performance Rust-based API that generates Apple Spatial Photos (HEIC) from standard 2D images using AI depth estimation, neural inpainting, and AWS Serverless architecture.
- 💡 You can explore my repositories below to see examples of my work, backend utilities, and custom web solutions.
- 🏢 I run my own business through [web-dream.tech](https://web-dream.tech/), collaborating with clients to bring their ideas to life from concept to deployment.
- 📫 How to reach me: Connect with me on [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/maciej-lewandowskii/) or via my [website](https://web-dream.tech/).
